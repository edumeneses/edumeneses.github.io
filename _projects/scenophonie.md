Scenophony names the art of shaping a collective immersive sound experience of variable geometry, accounting for the space, the stage, the aesthetics, and the sensory plurality of a mobile audience. I led this research and innovation project at the Société des Arts Technologiques (SAT), funded through the Programme de soutien aux organismes (PSO) of Quebec's Ministère de l'Économie, de l'Innovation et de l'Énergie (MEIE), and completed in 2025.

Most spatialization tools available today are built on an egocentric and idealized conception of space, whether through concentric surround formats or individual binaural listening. Consequently, creators rarely see alternatives to a convergent reproduction system, such as a dome, or an isolated one, such as headphones. Virtually no tool supports the integral design of a volumetric sound environment of variable geometry, in which divergent diffusion devices, for example the IKO by Sonible or the Audiodice developed at the SAT, are combined with a convergent peripheral system.

The project addressed two gaps simultaneously. First, we adapted and optimized an existing free and open source spatialization application to volumetric spatialization in asymmetric spaces of variable geometry, including fulldome venues such as the SAT's Satosphère and installations distributed across galleries, museums, and outdoor sites. Second, we worked on interoperable control between intermedia applications, since spatialization procedures are not directly compatible with the control parameters of other sensory modalities such as lighting, haptic response, or the position of objects in a three dimensional video engine. Both directions converge on a middleware requirement: bidirectional translation of spatial coordinates between heterogeneous systems, at variable scale.

The project ran from a market analysis and state of the art review, through the assembly of a dedicated immersive listening laboratory, to qualitative listening experiments with audiences from different backgrounds. Furthermore, we documented the resulting toolkit alongside the methodological and practical solutions, so that the receiving communities can adopt them quickly.
