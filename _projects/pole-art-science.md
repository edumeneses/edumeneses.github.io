The Pôle numérique art-science is a two-year programme (April 2026 to March 2028) funded by the Conseil des arts et des lettres du Québec (CALQ). Within the CALQ network of digital hubs (pôles numériques), the Société des arts technologiques (SAT) carries the art-science programme, and I wrote the application and lead the programme.

The programme rests on research-creation residencies, in which artists work with the SAT research team on spatial audio, intermedia sequencing, signal processing, and the Internet of Things (IoT). In 2026, we launched a cross-residency call with Les 7 Doigts (LAB7), whose mission is to host technological prototyping for the performing arts, so that each residency combines the stage expertise of the company with the immersive and technical environment of the SAT.

The programme also funds an annual series of training workshops for the other digital hubs in Quebec. Every tool and method it produces is shared through free software and open access, which supports the technological autonomy of artists across the province.
