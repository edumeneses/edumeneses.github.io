ArtIA is a collective initiative co-led by the Société des Arts Technologiques (SAT) with Sporobole and Projet collectif, aimed at establishing an infrastructure commons that lets Quebec cultural organizations develop their artificial intelligence tools collectively. I prepared the SAT component of the 2025 submission to the social innovation stream of the Programme de soutien aux organismes (PSO) of Quebec's Ministère de l'Économie, de l'Innovation et de l'Énergie (MEIE). The proposal was not retained for funding.

The initiative starts from a diagnosis of technological dependency. The prohibitive cost of commercial artificial intelligence solutions creates a digital divide inside the cultural sector; the systematic export of Quebec data to commercial systems trained on other values erodes cultural specificity, both linguistic and aesthetic; and the chronic instability of existing applications is particularly damaging in a sector where the continuity of projects and the longevity of works matter. In contrast with adopting commercial platforms wholesale, the initiative proposes collective use over individual ownership, participatory governance, and social innovation as the organizing principles.

The SAT experiment focused on artificial intelligence systems built on small data and small models. The objective was an open source toolkit, grounded in scientific research and research-creation, that lets artists run personalized models autonomously and ethically while favouring digital sobriety and the optimization of collective resources. Following the living lab methodology, the work was organized as co-design with practitioners and researchers, toolkit development emphasizing accessibility, modularity, and durability, mini-residencies pairing practitioners with researchers under ethnographic observation, and the documentation and dissemination of both the tools and the research-creation process.

Partners of the broader initiative include Sporobole, Projet collectif, mXlabs at Hexagram (UQAM), the Laboratoire Formes · Ondes at the Faculté de musique of the Université de Montréal, the Chaire de recherche du Québec sur l'intelligence artificielle et le numérique francophone at the Institut national de la recherche scientifique (INRS), LaCogency, Machine Agencies at Milieux (Concordia University), the Metacreation Lab (Simon Fraser University), the Laboratoire en innovation ouverte (LLio), Calcul Québec, and the Plateforme d'Innovation Numérique et Quantique du Québec (PINQ²).
